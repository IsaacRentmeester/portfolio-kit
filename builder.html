Portfolio Kit

Portfolio builder

Works with ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any AI chat. Your AI writes the words; this page turns them into your website. Nothing you add here is uploaded anywhere. It all stays on this computer.

1

Give your AI the instructions

Copy the prompt, open a new chat in ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude, paste it, and attach your résumé and your LinkedIn PDF (LinkedIn profile → More → Save to PDF). Answer its questions.

Show the prompt
2

Paste what your AI wrote

When your AI sends a block of JSON (it starts with {), copy the whole thing and paste it here. Pasting its full reply is fine too.

3

Add your photos optional

A headshot, plus up to three photos of you doing things you care about. They're shrunk to web size automatically.

4

Preview

Want changes? Tell your AI, paste its new JSON in step 2, and check again.

Your site shows up here after step 2.

5

Download and put it online

  1. Go to vercel.com/drop and sign in (free).
  2. Drag your zip from your Downloads onto that page. (If your Mac already unzipped it, drag the site folder instead.) Name the project firstname-lastname. Deploy.
  3. Copy your .vercel.app link and give it to your AI. It adds the link to your data so previews work. Paste the new JSON here, download again, and drop the new zip on the same project.
  4. Ask your AI: "Add it to my LinkedIn." It'll walk you through the Featured section and the link under your name.
